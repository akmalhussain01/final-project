About Us

Built by travellers, for travellers

Our story

Wanderlust Atlas started as a shared notebook of places worth the airfare. What began as recommendations passed between friends grew into a single guide covering the beaches, temples, and countries we kept returning to — and the ones still on our list.

Our mission

We believe good travel advice should come from people who've actually stood on the sand, climbed the temple steps, or gotten lost in the old town. Every recommendation on this site is chosen for what makes a place worth the trip, not just what photographs well.
